Undergraduate thesis · DLSU · 2026 – 2027 · Prototype in development

AI-Agentic Digital Marketing and Chat-Based App

Problem
A two-person, made-to-order eatery takes most of its orders through Facebook Messenger. Replies get missed or delayed, and orders are written by hand in a logbook.
What it does
A seller-side Android app linked to the eatery's Facebook Page. It is designed to sort customer chats into inquiries and orders, draft same-day pickup orders for staff to accept, and track the menu and daily order limits. Google Gemini classifies messages and extracts order details; replies use fixed templates. It also drafts weekly promotional posts that the team reviews and posts by hand.
My role
Team lead of four. I work on the Messenger chatbot back end and Supabase routing (Edge Functions, PostgreSQL), designed the system and process flowcharts, and coordinate the manuscript and adviser consultations.
  • TypeScript
  • React Native (Expo)
  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL
  • Google Gemini API
  • Meta Messenger Platform
  • Git & GitHub

A.I.M. (Archers Interactive Map)

Problem
New students have trouble finding their way around a large campus, and building information is scattered.
What it does
A non-GPS campus map for DLSU Manila and DLSU Laguna. It finds the fastest route between two buildings using Dijkstra's algorithm and shows information about each building.
My role
Team lead and sole developer: GUI design, front end and back end. The routes came out accurate, and the professor praised it as worth implementing on campus.
